This manuscript is an original article extracted from the author's own larger, openly published concept project on ethical guidelines for artificial consciousness, available under the DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21453666 (GitHub: github.com/saigkill/machine-consciousness, CC BY 4.0). The manuscript is substantially rewritten and refocused for journal publication: the argument is presented in a self-contained form, the sections on functional forgetting, the empirical memory literature, and the counter-position are newly developed for this paper, and all third-party sources cited are original scholarly publications. The research design and the use of AI-assisted drafting/translation are documented in the project's methodological reflection (research/methodological_reflection.md) in the linked repository). The author has no conflict of interest.
