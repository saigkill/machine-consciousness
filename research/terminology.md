# Terminology — Fixed Translations de ↔ en

Purpose: A fixed set of translations for the technical terms, consistent across all articles,
the concept, and the book projects. Entries are added as soon as a new term appears in an
article. Consistent terminology keeps the writing recognizable across years as a single
author — and prevents reviewer confusion from drifting terms.

Rule: A term is added only once it appears in at least two documents or is a core term of the
concept. Keep the established literature's wording where one side is canonical (e.g., keep
C_I/C_P untranslated).
